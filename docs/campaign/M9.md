# M9: experimental approximate Poisson leaping

`tau MODEL REQUEST` consumes `crn-craft.tau-request/0.1`; results use their own
`crn-craft.tau-result/0.1` and `approximate_ensemble` assessment. The embedded
reference_run carries exact-model/time/output/budget settings, not a claim that
the leap method is SSA. Legacy run/result/archive shapes remain unchanged.

Adaptive drift/variance-based steps use independent Poisson firings. Critical
low-count channels force an exact Direct event; negative or excessive gross
consumption proposals are rejected and the step halved, with Direct fallback
after 20 rejections. Populations are never clipped or reflected. This explicit
experimental algorithm can be biased by freezing/rejection; it is not the full
CGP critical-channel algorithm. Its result is never labelled exact.

Tau leaping draws from its own keyed stream (domain
`crn-craft.chacha8-tau/0.1`), with a separate algorithm identity.
Approximate paths may depend on the save grid. Event, leap, population, workspace,
output, cancellation and deadline limits apply. Incomplete ensembles withhold
statistics. Since P19 (algorithm `/0.3`), a leap mean of at most 16 uses the
multiplication method and a larger mean uses Hörmann's transformed rejection
PTRS (1993), which needs about 1.1 pairs of uniforms per variate for any mean.
Before P19 a large mean was split into chunks of at most 16, so a draw cost was
proportional to the expected firings. See `performance-p11-p20.md`.

All 30000 predeclared analytic control paths complete with non-negative counts.
CDF errors at epsilon 0.1 / 0.05 / 0.025 are 0.02382 / 0.00897 / 0.00712,
within the declared approximation-plus-sampling envelopes. Mean gates also pass;
normal/-O reports agree. Tests cover strict requests, reproducibility, cancellation,
event/leap limits and a critical homodimer's last reachable count. These small
controls do not establish accuracy for stiff or highly coupled biological models.
