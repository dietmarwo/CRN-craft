# Dynamic, attributed species (rules engine): K0/K1 protocol

Date: 2 November 2026. Plan: `claude/clonal-ssa-plan-2026-11-02.md` (fcmaes
project), revised after Axel Kowald asked for an implementation that handles
models beyond his mtDNA case. Committed before any K1 experiment.

## K0: contracts

- `crn-craft.rules-model/0.1`: species types with named numeric attributes,
  parameters, initial instances, count-weighted observables, rule schemata
  over one or two reactant patterns, optional stop condition.
- `crn-craft.rules-run/0.1`: model reference by SHA-256, time grid, ensemble,
  outputs and budgets, mirroring `ssa-run`.
- `crn-craft.rules-result/0.1`: per-replicate accounting, observable series,
  ensemble statistics, optional final instances.
- Schemas are generated from the Rust types; unknown fields and duplicate keys
  are rejected; every expression is parsed and name-checked before execution.

Semantics fixed now:

- An instance is a type plus a vector of attribute values (finite f64, -0
  normalized) and a copy number. Products with equal attributes merge into one
  instance; an instance is removed when its count reaches zero. `new_id()`
  yields a value never used before in the replicate, which makes a product a
  new clone.
- Unimolecular propensity: `global × local(x) × n_x`.
- Bimolecular propensity over two identical patterns (same type, condition and
  factor): `global × Σ over unordered pairs of distinct molecules of h(x)·h(y)`
  = `global × ½[(Σ h n)² − Σ h² n]` (the stochastic mass-action convention
  n(n−1)/2 for one instance). Over two patterns of different types:
  `global × (Σ h_x n_x)(Σ h_y n_y)`. Same-type patterns that differ are
  rejected in K1.
- `local`/factor expressions may use parameters and reactant attributes;
  `global` may use parameters and observables only; random draws (`bernoulli`,
  `choice`, `uniform`, `normal`, `new_id`) are allowed only in product
  attributes. No time-dependent rates in K1.
- Observable values and all propensity sums come from pairwise sum trees
  rebuilt from their leaves, so they depend only on the current state, not on
  history. A positive pattern weight below 2^-53 of its tree total is an error.
- After a stop condition becomes true, the state is held to the end of the
  report grid and the replicate is marked `stopped` with its stop time.

## K1: engine and validation targets

Engine: dynamic instances, one- and two-reactant schemata, observables,
global factors, keyed per-replicate ChaCha8 streams (own domain), ordered
parallel ensembles, event/sampling/instance/output budgets and cancellation.

Statistical gates (one family; family alpha 0.001; 100,000 replicates per
fixture; DKW one-sample bound `sqrt(ln(2K/alpha)/(2N))` with K the number of
marginal comparisons, each a CDF at one report time):

1. **Immigration–death** (`∅ → X` at 10, `X → ∅` at 1, X0 = 0): Poisson law
   with mean `10(1 − e^{−t})` at t = 0.5, 1, 3.
2. **Immigration–dimerization** (`∅ → X` at 20, `X + X → ∅` at 0.1, X0 = 0):
   exact master equation, truncated at 120 molecules with recorded leakage,
   at t = 0.5, 1, 3; the rules engine and CRN-craft's network engine are both
   compared with this law (embedding check).
3. **mtDNA, fixed population** (Kowald's Moran-like model with his
   competition term): K = 20, δ = 1, sw = 1, sm = 1.5, μ = 0.1,
   fracAdv = 0.5, w0 = 20; exact master equation on (wt, sw-mutants,
   sm-mutants) lumped over clones; marginals of wt, all mutants and
   sm-mutants at t = 1, 3, 10.
4. **Aggregation, constant kernel** (`X(a) + X(b) → X(a+b)` at 1, 12
   monomers): exact master equation over the 77 partitions of 12; marginals of
   the number of clusters and of the largest cluster at t = 0.02, 0.1, 0.5.
5. **Aggregation, multiplicative kernel** (rate `a·b`, 12 monomers): same
   construction and times.

A wrong-law power check per fixture (shifted parameters) must be rejected.
Unit tests cover expression parsing and name checks, merge and `new_id`
semantics, mass conservation in aggregation, replay across thread counts,
budgets, cancellation and strict input rejection. Failures are kept; no
threshold changes after a failure.

Not in K1: time-dependent rates and ODE coupling (K2, Kowald's
variable-population model), Python model builder (K4), compartments (K5).
